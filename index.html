SERPO — Sentence-level Entropy-Rewarded Policy Optimization

2025

Research internship · Falcon Reasoning team, Technology Innovation Institute (Abu Dhabi).

GRPO assigns the same advantage to every token of a chain-of-thought. SERPO introduces a sentence-granularity advantage bonus driven by step entropy: low-entropy sentences (filler) are discouraged, high-entropy reasoning steps are rewarded. The method preserves the GRPO update dynamics while matching the natural step structure of distilled reasoning models like DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen.

  • RL for reasoning
  • GRPO / DAPO
  • Entropy shaping
  • verl / Megatron

Half-denoising in score-based generative models

2025–26

MVA · Probabilistic Graphical Models project, ENS Paris-Saclay.

Annealed Langevin Dynamics suffers from a discretization bias that grows with the noise level. Building on Hyvärinen's half-denoising trick, we add a small correction to the score step inside an NCSN sampler and benchmark on MNIST, CIFAR-10 and CelebA. The modification recovers cleaner modes at high noise without losing exploration.

  • Score-based diffusion
  • Langevin dynamics
  • NCSN
  • PyTorch
